Why Memory Notes exists

And what it is for.

The purpose

Life stories, memories and ideas are easy to lose. Most of them arrive once, in passing, and are gone by the evening. Memory Notes exists to give them somewhere to land immediately, in as many words as they deserve.

What you can do here

Write a note in one of three categories — daily routine, memories, or something else — and keep it in a single grid you can read back at any time. Everything you write stays yours: only you can edit or delete your own notes.

How it is built

A Spring Boot application with Thymeleaf for the pages, HTMX for the small interactions that update parts of a page, and Bootstrap for the layout. Your account is protected with a token-based sign-in, and every server error is recorded so problems can be traced.
